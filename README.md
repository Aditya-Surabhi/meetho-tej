# meetho-tej

Everything behind the reels on [@meetho.tej](https://www.instagram.com/meetho.tej/), a series called **Deep Things**. Built in public.

Curious about the deep things in life, said out loud, in Telugu.

The Instagram page is the product. This repo is how I run it: the plan, the experiments, the decisions and what I learn each month.

## Product thinking

1. [Mission and Vision](Mission%20and%20Vision.md): why I am doing this.
2. [Content Plan](Content%20Plan.md): the account, the pillars, the format, the rules.
3. [Roadmap](roadmap.md): now, next, later.
4. [Experiments](experiments.md): one hypothesis per reel, results at the monthly check.
5. [Decision Log](decisions.md): what I decided, why, and the tradeoff.
6. [Build Log](build-log/): one entry a month.

## Research and scripts

1. [research/](research/): notes on other creators and formats I study.
2. `scripts/`: final scripts, one per reel. A script is added only after I have checked it and said it is final, so what you read there is what was said on camera.
