# Mission and Vision

## Mission

Think out loud about the deep things in life, in Telugu, in my own voice, so I remember what I learn and so someone else gets a word for something they already feel.

## Vision

**At Day 100:** inner strength and confidence that I can put myself out there. The target is about 28 reels. Even 20 is a win, because the real thing is that I showed up.

**Beyond:** a YouTube channel, started after Day 100. For the rest I do not have a name yet, and that is fine.

## Why

1. I read a lot and I discover things, and they stay in my head and I forget them. When I say them out loud, in the open, I remember. I get opinions I can share.
2. I want to become someone who finishes things in public, on a schedule, with my name and my face on them.
3. Telugu is where my real emotions live. So that is the language I think out loud in.

## The Voice

Calm. Specific. A little dry. A smile at the end. Quiet confidence, never a lecture. I ask questions more than I give answers.

## What This Is Not

1. Not a race against anyone's grid. The question "what better can I do" has no finish line, so I do not play it.
2. Not about followers, views or virality. The first audience is me.
3. Not a perfect reel. The first six are allowed to be mediocre.

## When I Feel Lost, Ask Three Questions

1. **Did I finish something this week?** Published is the only thing that counts.
2. **Did I say it out loud?** That is the point. Everything else is a bonus.
3. **Does it sound like me?** If yes, it is good enough.

If I am comparing, I replace "what better can I do" with "what can I finish by Sunday."

If I want to start something new, it goes in the parking lot. New ideas are procrastination wearing a nicer jacket.
