# Mission and Vision

*Rewritten November 16, 2026, from my own words. The first version (November 7) was me holding back. This one is true. Read it when I am lost.*

## Why (now)

1. **My daughter.** Finding out I am becoming a father opened parts of me I had never explored. I want to show her she can do anything she wants to do, and not just tell her. I want to show it by doing it. This is the biggest why.
2. **A different perspective.** People are listening to content about communicating and understanding people, and I care deeply about it. There is a real need for it in India, and especially where I come from. I want to show there is another way to see things, and wake up the thoughtful version of the person watching. I don't want to be a guru.
3. **I have what it takes, now.** With AI, one person can make content end to end. I have the face, I speak well, and I communicate well. The last piece is not being scared of the camera. I go there and talk to people.
4. **My exit plan.** I am 37. I want content to be the thing that means I don't have to work for someone else in the next 3-5 years. I want it to make a lot of money. I say that out loud, with a smile. When I chase something, the effort compounds and so does the return. I have seen it in my own life.

*Still true from before, as a bonus:* when I say what I read out loud, I remember it.

## Mission

Say the things people feel but rarely say out loud, in Telugu, so they see themselves and the people they love differently.

## Vision

**At Day 100 (February 14, 2027):** I have proven I can do it. About 28 reels published, no fear of the camera, a format that works, and the editing done mostly by my own AI workflow, so making a reel costs me hours, not days.

**Year one:** an audience that shares my content to people they love, a YouTube channel started after Day 100, and the first money from content.


## The Scoreboard (two phases)

Both are true: I want a lot of money, and a new account needs time. So I measure the right thing at the right time.

**Days 1 to 100, the habit phase.** I measure what I control:
1. Reels published, two a week.
2. A day I hit record without dread.
3. Shares and replies. Did anyone pass it on?
4. Time per reel, going down as the edit workflow improves.

**After Day 100, the growth phase.** Followers, reach, and then income get real targets, set at the Day 100 review using three months of data.

**Rules that protect the run:**
1. **No quit decisions on a bad day.** If I want to stop, I post one more reel and reread this file first. Quitting is only decided at the Day 100 review.

## The Voice

Calm. Specific. A little dry. A smile at the end. Quiet confidence, never a lecture. I ask questions more than I give answers.

## What This Is Not

1. Not a guru channel. I offer a perspective, not answers.
2. Not a race against anyone's grid. "What better can I do" has no finish line. "What can I finish by Sunday" does.
3. Not a perfect reel. The first six are allowed to be mediocre.
4. Not a place for my origin story. I say what I notice, never where it started.

## When I Feel Lost, Ask Three Questions

1. **Did I finish something this week?** Published is the only thing that counts.
2. **Would I be proud to show her this?**
3. **Does it sound like me?** If yes, it is good enough.
