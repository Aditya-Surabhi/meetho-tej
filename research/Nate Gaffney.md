# Research: Nate Gaffney (@nategaffney)

*Studied November 11, 2026. This is research on another creator's format. I study the shape, I do not compare myself, and I do not reuse his words or his type names.*

## What he posts (seen on his public profile)

1. One person talking to the camera, outdoors, natural light, tripod, a different outfit each time.
2. Each reel is built around a named type of person, shown as a cover title.
3. Single words appear on screen as he says them.
4. Hand gestures throughout, so it reads as conversation.
5. Highlights like "Start a Series" and "Journal Entries".

## What the Creative Mode breakdown says

Source: Martin Wang, Creative Mode, "How Nate Gaffney Went From 10K to 910K Followers in 91 Days", https://www.joincreativemode.com/library/nategaffney. These are that analyst's numbers about one creator, in English, with an American audience. I read about the first third of the article.

1. He grew from 10K to 910K followers in 91 days (Jul 29 to Oct 28, 2026), per the article.
2. The change was not gear or location. He stopped making videos about himself and started making videos about the viewer and a friend they picture, then gave them a reason to send it.
3. About 95% of his views came from videos that describe a type of person. Tips and pep talks did very little.
4. The hook "There's a rare breed of person who is both X and Y" accounted for about 60% of his views. "Confession" hooks and direct pep talks did very little.
5. A large share of his views came from one recurring series.
6. The closing line invites the viewer to send the video to someone and tell them they're great.

## What I'm taking

1. The shape: a type of person, two traits that don't usually go together, described warmly, then a send line.
2. A named series, with each episode self contained.
3. One word at a time on screen.

## What I'm leaving

1. His type names and his wording.
2. His numbers as a promise. One creator is a model for a shape, not a forecast.
3. His English only delivery. Mine is Telugu.
