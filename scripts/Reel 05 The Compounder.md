## Final script (what I say)

Virat Kohli, Leo Messi, inka chala mandi athletes andari lo oka chinna trait common ga vuntundi. Ade ento telusa?

Welcome to Deep Things. Peru teleyani feelings gurunchi oka chinna series. Ee roju, the compounders.

Vallini chuste, enta lucky oo anukuntam. Kakapote aa luck venakala chala hardwork and daily routine vuntundi.

Chala chinna ga start chestaru, results meeda peddaga focus pettaru, effort meeda pedtaru. Oka roju miss ayina, next day malli start chestaru and they believe before they see.

Illanti curious topics meeda interest unte, please follow me and next episode lo kaludam.

## Shot plan

*The plan from before the shoot, kept as it was. On November 25 I filmed each line as its own clip, with retakes, and filmed no cutaway clips.*

1. **Opening:** talk to the lens from the first frame, with the names. Warm, curious. Leave a small beat after "Ade ento telusa?" so the next line lands as the answer.
2. **"Welcome to Deep Things":** a small smile. It's the series line, so keep it light.
3. **Slow down for "aa luck venakala hardwork and everyday routine vuntundi."** It's the turn of the reel.
4. **"they believe before they see":** say it quietly. It's the last idea, so let it land.
5. **Follow line:** easy and friendly, like inviting a friend to the next one. After "kaludam", hold a smile for a second while the card comes up.
6. **Takes:** two full takes. The editor keeps the one that sounds natural.

No cutaway clips are planned.
