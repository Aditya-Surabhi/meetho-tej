"(clear-ga)" is optional: I decide on camera. Updated November 20, 2026: added "పెద్దవాళ్ళు emaina cheppaka," the line I said on camera. ## Final script (what I say)

Mee intlo eppudaina idi observe chesara?

Dinner table దగ్గర, పెద్దవాళ్ళు emaina cheppaka, అందరూ "సరే" అంటారు, kani ఒక్క person మాత్రం calm ga "Nenu idi different ga alochistunnanu" antaru.

Ala enduku anipistundo clear ga cheptaru. Peddavalla maata kuda complete ga vintaru.

Vallu aa idea tho disagree avutunnaru, kani peddavallatho kaadu, meetho kaadu.

Vallaki argument gelavadam mukhyam kaadu. Manasulo maata clear ga cheppadam mukhyam.

Idi Deep Things. Ee roju manam the kind disagreer gurinchi telusukunnamu.

Ilanti peru teliyani feelings ni ardham chesukovalanukunte, follow me.

## On screen (English, 2 to 4 words at a time, as I say them)

1. Have you ever / observed this at home?
2. At the dinner table, / when the elders / say anything, / everyone says okay. / But one person / calmly says, / "I'm thinking about this / differently."
3. Why they feel / that way, / they explain clearly. / And they hear / the elders out, / completely.
4. They disagree / with that idea, / but not with elders, / not with *you*. (the italic keyword)
5. Winning the argument / doesn't matter to them. / Speaking their heart clearly / matters.
6. This is Deep Things. / Today we learned about / **The Kind Disagreer** (title, as I say it; also the cover)
7. To understand / nameless feelings / like these, / follow me.
8. Sign-off card, silent: మీతో. మీ తేజ. · MEETHO · MEE TEJA · @meetho.tej

## Shot plan

*The plan from before the shoot, kept as it was. On November 20 I filmed each line as its own clip, said "clear ga", and filmed no cutaway clips.*

1. **Opening:** talk to the lens from the first frame, with the question.
2. **Calm on the quote.** Say "Naaku konchem different ga anipistundi" softly, the way the person would.
3. **Lean in** on "Meetho kaadu."
4. **Slow down** for "Manasulo maata cheppadam mukhyam." It's the line that lands.
5. **Takes:** one with "(clear-ga)" and one without. The editor keeps the one that sounds natural.
6. **End:** after "follow cheyyandi", hold a smile for a second while the card comes up.
