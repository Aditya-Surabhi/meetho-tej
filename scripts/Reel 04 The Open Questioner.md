# Reel 4 Final: The Open Questioner

*Made November 25, 2026, from my Draft 12, built with Claude from my own words. The "answer" wording is used all the way through (my choice). No body line, no "why" question, no "kopam taggutundi" line. I check the Telugu when I film. If I change words on camera, this script changes first.*

**Series:** Deep Things. **Pillar:** How people connect. **Length:** about 38 s (estimate, before filming).
**Format:** talking head, a type of person, one idea explained as a chain (question, slower reaction, clear thinking, my own answer), send line, follow line. No spoken sign-off; the sign-off card stays.

---

## Final script (what I say)

Chala mandi problem vinagane, fix cheyadaniki chustaru. Kani na favourite people open questions adugutaru.

Vallaki answer ivvalani hurry undadu. Meeke answer telusu ani nammutaru.

Eroju Deep Things series lo, The Open Questioner.

Ee questions chala simple ga untaie: What happened, How did it happen?

Avi mana reactions ni slow chestaie. Slow ayite, manam clear ga think chestam.

Clear ga think chesinappudu, answer manake dorukutundi. Aa answer manaki resonate avutundi. And most importantly, you feel heard.

మీ life లో ఇలాంటి person మీకుంటే, send them this video. మరి అది మీరే అయితే, take it as a compliment.

Elanti peru teliyani feelings ni ardam chesukovalante, follow me!

## On screen (English, 2 to 4 words at a time, as I say them)

1. Most people / hear a problem / and try to fix it. / My favourite people / ask open questions.
2. They're not in a hurry / to give an answer. / They trust / you already have it.
3. This is Deep Things. / **The Open Questioner** (title, as I say it; also the cover)
4. These questions / are simple: / What happened? / How did it happen?
5. They slow / our reactions. / Slow down, / and we think clearly.
6. Thinking clearly, / the answer comes / from us. / It resonates. / And most of all, / you feel *heard*. (the italic keyword)
7. Know someone like this? / Send them this video. / If it's you, / take it as a compliment.
8. To understand / nameless feelings / like these, / follow me.
9. Sign-off card, silent: మీతో. మీ తేజ. · MEETHO · MEE TEJA · @meetho.tej

---

## Shot plan

1. **Opening:** talk to the lens from the first frame, with "Chala mandi problem vinagane…". Calm and warm.
2. **Soften on "Kani na favourite people".** A small smile, the tone of someone describing a friend they love.
3. **Slow down** for "What happened, How did it happen?" Say the two questions gently, the way the person would ask them.
4. **Let "you feel heard" land.** Pause half a beat before it, and let the voice drop, not rise.
5. **Send line:** straight down the lens, with a smile.
6. **End:** after "follow me", hold the smile for a second while the card comes up.
7. **Takes:** two full takes, one with a slightly higher energy. The editor keeps the one that sounds natural.

No cutaway clips are planned. Reel 3 was shot as a plain talking head too.
