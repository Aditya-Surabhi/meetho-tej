# Reel 4 Final: The Open Questioner

*Made November 25, 2026, from my Draft 12, built with Claude from my own words. The "answer" wording is used all the way through (my choice). No body line, no "why" question, no "kopam taggutundi" line. I check the Telugu when I film. If I change words on camera, this script changes first. Updated November 25, 2026, after editing: the script, on-screen text and caption now match v1, as I said them on camera. "Manaki" (us) in place of "Meeke", "Ee questions" in place of "Avi", the longer clear-thinking lines, "we feel heard", and the compliment line all in English. The title is now a heading at the top, in a white box.*

**Series:** Deep Things. **Pillar:** How people connect. **Length:** 46 s (v1).
**Format:** talking head, a type of person, one idea explained as a chain (question, slower reaction, clear thinking, my own answer), send line, follow line. No spoken sign-off; the sign-off card stays.

---

## Final script (what I say)

Chala mandi problem vinagane, daanni fix cheyadaniki chustaru. Kani na favourite people open questions adugutaru.

Vallaki answer ivvalani hurry undadu. Manaki answer telusu ani nammutaru.

Eroju Deep Things series lo, The Open Questioner.

Ee questions chala simple ga untaie: What happened, How did it happen?

Ee questions mana reactions ni slow chestaie. Slow ayite, manam clear ga think chestam.

Clear ga think chesinappudu, manaki answers dorukutai. Aa answer kuda manaki baaga resonate avutundi. And most importantly, we feel heard.

మీ life లో ఇలాంటి person మీకుంటే, send them this video. If this is you, take it as a compliment.

Elanti peru teliyani feelings ni ardam chesukovalanukunte, follow me!

## On screen (English, 2 to 4 words at a time, as I say them)

1. Most people / hear a problem / and try to fix it. / But my favourite people / ask open questions.
2. They're not in a hurry / to give an answer. / They trust / we already have it.
3. This is Deep Things. (The heading **The Open Questioner** sits at the top in a white box, from the first frame through "take it as a compliment". The cover keeps the full-size title.)
4. These questions / are simple: / What happened? / How did it happen?
5. These questions / slow our reactions. / Slow down, / and we think clearly.
6. Thinking clearly, / we find the answers. / That answer, too, / really resonates with us. / And most importantly, / we feel *heard*. (the italic keyword)
7. Know someone like this? / Send them this video. / If this is you, / take it as a compliment.
8. To understand / nameless feelings / like these, / follow me.
9. Sign-off card, silent: మీతో. మీ తేజ. · MEETHO · MEE TEJA · @meetho.tej

## Shot plan

*The plan from before the shoot, kept as it was. On November 25 I filmed each line as its own clip, with retakes, and filmed no cutaway clips.*

1. **Opening:** talk to the lens from the first frame, with "Chala mandi problem vinagane…". Calm and warm.
2. **Soften on "Kani na favourite people".** A small smile, the tone of someone describing a friend they love.
3. **Slow down** for "What happened, How did it happen?" Say the two questions gently, the way the person would ask them.
4. **Let "you feel heard" land.** Pause half a beat before it, and let the voice drop, not rise.
5. **Send line:** straight down the lens, with a smile.
6. **End:** after "follow me", hold the smile for a second while the card comes up.
7. **Takes:** two full takes, one with a slightly higher energy. The editor keeps the one that sounds natural.

No cutaway clips are planned. Reel 3 was shot as a plain talking head too.
