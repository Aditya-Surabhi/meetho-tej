## Two Pillars (pick one, never stare at a blank page)

1. **How people work** (inside a person): psychology, wiring, types. Why someone is the way they are.
2. **How people connect** (between people): how we talk, listen and show up for each other, in friendship, love and family.

They map to the mission: "see themselves" is pillar 1, "the people they love" is pillar 2. How to live on purpose and Body and mind are parked in the [Parking Lot](ideas.md#parking-lot).

**The lens across both (added November 9, 2026, from my wife):** many Telugu men rarely talk about the deep emotional side of things. I can be one who does it out loud, in Telugu, and gives other people the courage. This is not a third pillar. It is how every pillar gets said: honestly, quietly, with feeling, never as a lecture.

## Reel Format (25 to 45 seconds, spoken, one idea)

Phone, a cheap tripod, window light. No ring light, no mic, no setup to buy first.

**Self contained.** From reel 2 on, a topic starts and ends in the same reel. No "Next:" lines, no cliffhangers, no reel that needs another reel.

**Delivery: storytelling.** A small story about a type of person, tied to my own experience:

1. **Name the type.** A rare person who is both X and Y.
2. **A person doing a specific thing.** Not a concept. Someone viewers recognise in one line.
3. **The type is a compliment.** Someone to celebrate, not a problem to fix.
4. **One personal line** from my own life, in my own words.
5. **The send line.** "If you know someone like this, send them this."

**Our flavour on top of the shape:** Telugu and Tenglish, the series line said aloud near the end ("Idi Deep Things. Ee roju, ..."), the send line, a follow line, then the silent sign-off card.

**On screen (the text added in the edit):** short English captions, one word or phrase at a time as I say it, so non Telugu viewers can follow. The cover title names the type (for example "The Soft Strong One") and is what shows in the grid. No day counter on screen. The 100 days are my target, not part of the reel.

## Rules for Finishing

1. One account, one format, 100 days. No third thing. New ideas go in the [Parking Lot](ideas.md#parking-lot).
2. A reel is done only when it is published.
3. The first six reels are allowed to be mediocre. In writing.
4. Batch: record two reels in one sitting, post across the week.
5. Stats are checked once a month. The first check is December 9, 2026. Stats are feelings with decimals. A slow first month is expected, not a verdict.
6. Credit the source of any framework that is not mine.
7. Studying a working format is allowed. Comparing myself is not. One account at a time, about 20 minutes, and I leave with written beats (what is on screen, what the first line does, how it ends). Then I make my own in Telugu, in my voice, with my moment. If I leave thinking "I'm not as good", I stop and go record.
8. Not yet: my daughter and the origin story stay out of the reels for now. Reels say what I noticed, not where it started. The story will come out some day, when I choose.
9. Every reel closes its own topic. The hook opens a gap, the same reel closes it.
10. A script is final only when I say it is final. Only then does it go into [scripts/](scripts/).
