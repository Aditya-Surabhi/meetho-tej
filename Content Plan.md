# Content Plan

*Public edition. Updated November 11, 2026.*

## The Goal

100 days. Two reels a week, about 28 in total. Not followers, not virality. Twenty eight finished things with my face and my name on them, and the habit of saying what I learn out loud so it stays with me.

## The Account

**Platform:** Instagram reels only. YouTube is a later decision, made at Day 100.

**Handle:** @meetho.tej. Sign-off on every reel: "మీతో. మీ తేజ."

**Series name on screen:** Deep Things.

**Language:** Telugu. It's where my real emotions live. English text on screen so non Telugu viewers can follow. Scripts are drafted in English, then said in Telugu in my own words.

**What it is:** a curious person thinking out loud about the deep things in life. One idea per reel.

**Voice:** calm, specific, a little dry, a smile at the end. Quiet confidence, not a lecture.

## Four Pillars

1. **How people work:** psychology, wiring, types.
2. **How to live on purpose:** identity, values, time.
3. **People and love:** friendship, connection, family.
4. **Body and mind:** sport, flow, focus.

**The lens across all four:** many Telugu men rarely talk about the deep emotional side of things. I can be one who does it out loud, in Telugu, and gives other people permission. This is not a sixth pillar. It is how every pillar gets said: honestly, quietly, with feeling, never as a lecture.

## Reel Format (25 to 45 seconds, spoken, one idea)

Phone, a cheap tripod, window light. No ring light, no mic, no setup to buy first.

**Self contained.** From reel 2 on, a topic starts and ends in the same reel. No "Next:" lines, no cliffhangers, no reel that needs another reel.

**Delivery: storytelling.** A small story about a type of person, tied to my own experience:

1. **A person doing a specific thing.** Not a concept. Someone viewers recognise in one line.
2. **The type is a compliment.** Someone to celebrate, not a problem to fix.
3. **Describe them** in a few short lines, so the viewer pictures a real person they know.
4. **One personal line** from my own life, in my own words.
5. **The send line.** "If you know someone like this, send them this."

**Hook shape:** a rare type of person with two traits that do not usually go together ("A person who is both X and Y"). See `research/Nate Gaffney.md`.

**On screen:** one word at a time as I say it, a cover title that names the type, "Day __ of 100" at the start.

## Rules for Finishing

1. One account, one format, 100 days. No third thing. New ideas go in a parking lot.
2. A reel is done only when it is published.
3. The first six reels are allowed to be mediocre. In writing.
4. Batch: record two reels in one sitting, post across the week.
5. Stats are checked once a month, on the 9th, and not before. A new account takes time, so a slow first month is expected.
6. Credit the source of any framework that is not mine.
7. Studying a working format is allowed. Comparing myself is not. One account at a time, about 20 minutes, written beats, then I make my own in my voice. If I leave thinking "I'm not as good", I stop and go record.
8. Every reel closes its own topic.
9. A reel gets a final script only when I say the draft is final.

## How I Measure Progress

My scoreboard is not views. It is:

1. Reels published.
2. A day I hit record without dread.
3. One idea I still remember a week later because I said it out loud.
4. One person who replied or messaged.
