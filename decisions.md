# Decision Log

Each entry: the decision, why, and the tradeoff I accepted.

## November 6, 2026: Instagram reels only, YouTube decided at Day 100

**Why:** one platform, one format, so I finish things. **Tradeoff:** a smaller reach now. I accept it.

## November 6, 2026: Telugu spoken, English on screen

**Why:** Telugu is where my real emotions live, and the point is my own voice. **Tradeoff:** a narrower audience than English, and drafts need care so they sound like me.

## November 9, 2026: Every reel closes its own topic

**Why:** a viewer who lands on any single reel gets a whole idea. I can post in any order and skip a week without breaking anything. **Tradeoff:** no cliffhangers to pull people into the next reel.

## November 9, 2026: Check stats once a month, not weekly

**Why:** a new account has too little data for weekly checks to mean anything, and early numbers are noise. **Tradeoff:** slower learning. I accept it to protect consistency.

## November 11, 2026: Adopt the type of person format, with one personal line

**Why:** after studying one creator's public format and an analyst's breakdown, the pattern was clear: a type of person, two traits, a send line. I kept one line of my own so it still sounds like me. **Tradeoff:** the evidence is one creator, in English. It is a model for a shape, not a forecast.

## November 11, 2026: Build in public

**Why:** to show how I think about a product, with a plan, experiments and decisions in the open. **Tradeoff:** more exposure. Personal and private matters stay out, only the product thinking is shared.

## November 11, 2026: Cut from five pillars to four

**Why:** five topic areas was too much to hold in my head for a 100 day run, and a wide menu makes it easier to stall. The pillar I cut, taste (wine, art, aesthetics), is the one furthest from the rest. **Tradeoff:** a narrower range of topics. It is parked, not deleted, and can come back after the first month if the four feel easy.
