# Roadmap

*Updated November 11, 2026. The product is the @meetho.tej page. The user is someone who wants a word for something they already feel.*

## Now (Days 1 to 30)

1. Ship two reels a week in the Deep Things format.
2. Reel 2: "The Soft Strong One", in the type of person format.
3. First monthly metrics check on December 9, 2026.
4. Edit agent v1: raw take in, captioned reel out, so editing stops being the bottleneck.

## Next (Days 30 to 70)

1. Keep what the monthly checks say is working, drop what isn't.
2. Test one variable at a time: hook, cover title, length.
3. Decide the cover style so the series is recognisable in the grid.

## Later (Days 70 to 100)

1. Review: did I ship consistently, and did anything get shared or replied to?
2. Set growth and income targets for year one from three months of data.
3. Decide on YouTube at Day 100, not before.

## Not on the roadmap

1. A second account, a second format, or a new platform. New ideas go in a parking lot.
